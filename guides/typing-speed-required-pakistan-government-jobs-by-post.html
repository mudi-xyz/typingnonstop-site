Typing speed required for Pakistan government jobs, by post

If you are applying for a government post in Pakistan, the advert almost always names a typing speed. The number depends on the post, the department and sometimes the language, so it helps to see the pattern across many adverts before you decide how much to practise.

How to read the advert

  • BPS or BS. Federal posts are listed as BPS and provincial ones as BS, followed by a grade number. The grade matters less to the typing test than the title of the post.
  • WPM or KDPH. Most adverts give words per minute, and data entry adverts often give key depressions per hour. Divide KDPH by 300 for a rough WPM figure, as explained in our units guide.
  • English, Urdu or both. Some adverts name a language, and some ask for both English and Urdu typing.
  • On computer. Many adverts state computer typing, which means a keyboard test on screen and not a typewriter.

These are examples from published adverts, not a rule book. Adverts change from year to year and from department to department, so always use the figure in your own advert.

What the test usually checks

  • Speed and accuracy. Most tests count correct words only, which is net WPM, so mistakes lower your score. See net versus gross WPM.
  • Qualifying or marked. Some tests are a simple pass or fail at the required speed. Others, such as the ECP example, give marks for the typing test as well.
  • Office software. Many clerk tests add MS Word and Excel. Read typing test and MS Office skills test.

How much to practise

Aim to be comfortably above the figure, not exactly at it. A candidate who needs 30 WPM should practise until 35 to 40 WPM net is routine, because nerves and an unfamiliar keyboard usually cost a few words. Use a 15-minute daily routine and check your progress with timed tests at your advert's length. For country-by-country comparisons, see typing test pass marks by country.

Frequently asked questions

What typing speed is needed for a junior clerk job in Pakistan?

Many adverts ask for 30 words per minute, often on computer. Some ask for more, so check your own advert and aim to be comfortably above it.

Is data entry operator typing speed higher than clerk typing speed?

Often yes. One ECP advert screened data entry operators at 40 WPM in English, and another advert asked for 35 WPM and 10,000 key depressions per hour.

Do Pakistani typing tests include Urdu typing?

Some do. Certain adverts ask for English and Urdu typing together, so read the advert to see which languages are tested.
