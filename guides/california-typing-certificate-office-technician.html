California state typing certificate: 40 WPM in 5 minutes

California state government clerical jobs such as Office Assistant (Typing) and Office Technician (Typing) ask applicants for a typing certificate. The rules are specific, and they differ from the quick online tests many people take for practice, so it helps to know what the certificate must show before you pay for or sit a test.

What the state asks for

According to the California Department of Human Resources (CalHR), a typing certification is required for state clerical classes such as Office Assistant (Typing) and Office Technician (Typing), and the specific requirement is stated in the class specification. Postings for Office Technician (Typing) say the applicant must type at least 40 net words per minute from ordinary manuscript or printed material.

The certificate rules seen in job postings

  • Five-minute test. The certificate must come from a test that is five minutes long.
  • Proctored. Recent postings say the test must be proctored, either in person or virtually.
  • Recent. State postings say they honour certificates issued within the last four years.
  • Net speed. The 40 WPM is a net figure, so mistakes count against you.
  • Approved source. The certificate must meet CalHR requirements, and CalHR lists the approved providers.

Some city and county employers set their own rules. One typist clerk listing asked for a certificate of 40 net WPM obtained within the last 12 months, so always read the posting you are applying to.

Where to check the current rules

Rules and approved providers change over time. Before you book a test, check the CalHR typing certification page and the job posting, and confirm the length, the proctoring requirement and how recent the certificate must be.

What TypingNonStop can and cannot do

TypingNonStop does not issue certificates and is not an approved provider. It can help you reach the speed, and you then take the official, proctored test. Do not use a practice result as a certificate.

How to prepare for a 5-minute test

  • Practise five-minute runs. A one-minute burst hides how fatigue changes your speed, so use full five-minute tests.
  • Aim above 40 net. Practise until 45 to 50 net WPM is routine, because test-day nerves usually cost a few words.
  • Watch accuracy. Net WPM falls quickly with errors. See how to reduce mistakes and net versus gross WPM.
  • Rehearse the setup. Practise with the keyboard and screen setup you expect, as described in the keyboard guide.
  • Plan the day. Use the test-day checklist.

Frequently asked questions

How fast must I type for a California Office Technician (Typing) job?

Job postings say at least 40 net words per minute, shown on a typing certificate.

How long is a California typing certificate valid?

State postings say they honour certificates issued within the last four years, but some local employers set shorter limits, so read the posting.

Can I use a free online typing test result as my certificate?

Not usually. Postings describe a five-minute, proctored test that meets CalHR requirements, so check the approved sources before you test.
