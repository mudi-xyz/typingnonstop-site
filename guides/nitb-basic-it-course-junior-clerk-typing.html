Junior clerk adverts with a 3-week NITB IT course and 30 WPM

If you read federal junior clerk adverts in Pakistan, you will often see two requirements side by side: a minimum typing speed and a three-week basic IT training course that includes MS Office, conducted by NITB, the National Information Technology Board. Candidates are often unsure whether they need the course before applying, and this guide explains what the adverts actually say.

What the adverts say

  • A Pakistan Railways headquarters advert for a Junior Clerk listed matriculation, a minimum typing speed of 30 WPM, and a three-week basic IT training course including MS Office conducted by NITB.
  • A federal revenue headquarters advert listed a minimum typing speed of 30 WPM and said the three-week basic IT course must be completed before the end of the probation period, otherwise the service could end.

The two examples are worded differently. One lists the course among the qualifications, while the other makes it a condition to complete after appointment. That difference is why you should read your own advert closely and not rely on a general summary.

What this means for you

  • The typing requirement is the first filter. Thirty WPM is the figure that appears in these adverts, and a test usually decides whether you meet it.
  • The course may or may not be needed before you apply. Check whether your advert says before appointment or within probation.
  • MS Office skills help in both cases. The course includes MS Office, so basic Word and Excel work will not be new.

What to do before you apply

  1. Read the whole advert, including the qualification column and any notes below it.
  2. Find out from the department or from NITB how enrolment works, since this guide cannot cover it.
  3. Check the typing speed in the advert and practise until you are comfortably above it.
  4. Prepare basic Word skills such as formatting, tables and printing, and simple Excel work such as entering data and sums.

Preparing for the typing part

At 30 WPM, accuracy matters as much as speed because most tests count only correct words. Practise with timed passages, see how to reduce mistakes, and use the 40 WPM plan if you are starting from a lower speed. A typical test day may include an unfamiliar keyboard, so also read the keyboard guide. Other speeds by post are in typing speed required for Pakistan government jobs.

Frequently asked questions

What is the NITB basic IT course in junior clerk adverts?

Some federal adverts ask for a three-week basic IT training course, including MS Office, conducted by NITB. Check your advert for whether it is needed before or after appointment.

What typing speed do these adverts ask for?

The examples we reviewed asked for a minimum of 30 words per minute.

Do I need the course before I apply?

It depends on the advert. One example listed it as a qualification and another allowed completion before the end of probation, so read yours carefully.
