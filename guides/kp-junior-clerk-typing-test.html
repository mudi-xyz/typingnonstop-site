KP Junior Clerk (BPS-11) typing test: how fast do you need to type?

If you are preparing for a Junior Clerk post in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, the typing test is often the round that decides who moves forward. Public-sector adverts for this post, such as those published through ETEA and for institutions like KMU, ask for a typing speed of 30 words per minute along with working knowledge of MS Office.

The number to aim for

The figure that appears in these adverts is 30 words per minute (WPM) for BPS-11 Junior Clerk posts. Every advert is different, so read the one you are applying to: the department can change the speed, the language, or the time allowed.

A word in a typing test is counted as five characters, including spaces. So 30 WPM is 150 characters a minute, or about two and a half keystrokes every second, sustained for the whole test.

Why practise at 35 or more

Test day is not like practice day. Nerves, an unfamiliar keyboard and an unfamiliar passage can cost you several words per minute. If your comfortable speed in practice is exactly 30, you have no safety margin. A sensible target is 35 WPM with at least 95 percent accuracy, so that a bad day still clears the line.

Net speed matters more than raw speed

Typing fast and making mistakes does not help. Many tests judge your net speed, which is your speed after mistakes are taken into account. See our guide on net WPM versus gross WPM for a worked example.

A simple weekly plan

  • Days 1 to 3: accuracy only. Type slowly and aim for fewer than two mistakes per hundred words.
  • Days 4 to 7: raise the speed by about five WPM while keeping accuracy above 95 percent.
  • Every day: ten minutes on the words you get wrong. Repeating your own mistakes is the fastest way to remove them.
  • Twice a week: a full timed test on an office-style passage with dates, numbers and punctuation.

On test day

Arrive early, adjust your seat and screen, and type the first lines slowly to settle your rhythm. Do not look at the keys, do not chase the clock, and do not stop to fix one mistake while the time runs out. If the test allows backspace, use it sparingly: every correction costs time.

Frequently asked questions

What is the typing speed for Junior Clerk in KPK?

Public adverts for BPS-11 Junior Clerk posts in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa commonly mention 30 words per minute, together with MS Office knowledge. Always confirm with the exact advert for your post.

Is 30 WPM gross or net?

It depends on the department and how the test is scored. Practise for net speed (correct words only) so you are safe either way.

How long does it take to reach 35 WPM?

It varies a lot between people. Short daily practice with a focus on accuracy usually helps more than occasional long sessions.
