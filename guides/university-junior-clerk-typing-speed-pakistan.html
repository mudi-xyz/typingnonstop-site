University junior clerk jobs in Pakistan: 30 WPM and the test

Universities in Pakistan regularly advertise Junior Clerk posts at BPS-11 or BS-11. The typing requirement is usually modest, but the details differ between universities, and knowing them early lets you prepare for the right test.

What the adverts usually say

  • Typing speed. A speed of 30 words per minute appears in many university adverts, in some cases in Urdu or English and in some cases in English only.
  • Education. Adverts commonly ask for matriculation or intermediate with second division, and a few ask for a bachelor's degree.
  • Computer skills. Phrases such as adequate computer literacy or proficiency in MS Office are common.
  • A typing certificate. Some adverts ask for a certificate of typing speed from a recognised institute, while others test you on the day.
  • Age. An upper age limit of 35 appears in several adverts, with relaxations that depend on the rules.

A higher figure appears now and then. One university advert asked for 40 WPM for a clerk post, so do not assume 30 without checking.

What the test often looks like

Published lists from university recruitment show candidates qualifying for a test of typing speed on computer in English and proficiency in MS Office. In other words, the typing test is often one of two practical stages, and it may come before an interview.

  • Typing passage. A passage in English, sometimes with Urdu as a second part, is typed in a set time.
  • MS Word. Formatting a short document, such as setting font, margins and alignment.
  • MS Excel. Entering data, simple formulas and basic formatting.

If an advert asks for a certificate

  1. Read exactly what the advert says about the issuing institute, for example whether it must be recognised.
  2. Check the date rule, because some employers want a recent certificate.
  3. Ask the university's recruitment office if anything is unclear, and keep a copy of every document.
  4. Keep practising even with a certificate, because the university may still test you.

TypingNonStop does not issue certificates. It helps you reach and keep the speed, and you can then take the official test or certificate course the advert names.

How to prepare in three weeks

  • Week 1. Test yourself for five minutes. If you are under 30 WPM net, work on finger placement and accuracy first.
  • Week 2. Practise 15 minutes a day, as in the daily routine guide, and aim for 35 WPM net at 95 percent accuracy or better.
  • Week 3. Add MS Word and Excel basics and rehearse a full test day with the test-day checklist.

Frequently asked questions

How fast must I type for a university junior clerk job?

Many Pakistani university adverts ask for 30 words per minute, but a few ask for more. Check the advert for your post.

Do universities ask for a typing certificate?

Some adverts do, from a recognised institute, while others hold a typing test on the day. Read the advert and ask the recruitment office if unsure.

Is there an MS Office test for junior clerks?

Often yes. Lists from university recruitment mention a stage that tests typing speed on computer and MS Office skills.
