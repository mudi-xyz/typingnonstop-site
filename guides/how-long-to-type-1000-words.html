How long does it take to type 1,000 words? WPM table

How long will a document take to type? The answer depends on your speed in words per minute, and the arithmetic is simple. This page gives ready-made figures, and then explains why real work usually takes longer.

The formula

Minutes needed equals the number of words divided by your WPM. For 1,000 words at 40 WPM, that is 1,000 divided by 40, which is 25 minutes.

Time to type 1,000 words and one page

The page column assumes about 500 words, which is a common estimate for a single-spaced page. A double-spaced page is usually around 250 to 300 words, so halve the time for that.

Why real documents take longer

  • Net, not gross. The table uses your net speed. Errors and corrections add time, as explained in net versus gross WPM.
  • Copying versus composing. Typing from a page is faster than writing your own text, because you must also think of what to say.
  • Formatting and checking. Headings, tables, spacing and proofreading add time on top of typing.
  • Numbers and symbols. Text with many digits and punctuation slows most typists.
  • Fatigue. Your speed over an hour is lower than over a minute.

As a rough planning habit, many people add 25 to 50 percent to the typing time for proofreading and formatting.

Characters and keystrokes

A typing-test word is five characters, so 1,000 words is about 5,000 keystrokes. At 30 WPM that equals 9,000 keystrokes per hour, which is why a 10,000 KPH requirement is only a little above 33 WPM. See WPM, CPM and KPH explained for the conversions.

Using this for job tests

If a job asks for 30 WPM, you will need about 33 minutes to type 1,000 words, which is useful when judging how long a typing test passage will take. To work out your own speed, see how to calculate words per minute, and to raise it, follow the 30-day plan.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to type 1,000 words at 40 WPM?

About 25 minutes of pure typing, before any proofreading or formatting.

How many words is one page?

Roughly 500 words for a single-spaced page and about 250 to 300 for a double-spaced page, though fonts and margins change this.

Why does real typing take longer than the table says?

Errors, thinking time, formatting, proofreading, digits and fatigue all add time to the pure typing estimate.
