Data entry operator jobs in Pakistan: KDPH and WPM explained

Data entry operator adverts in Pakistan do not all use the same unit. Some ask for words per minute, some ask for key depressions per hour (KDPH), and some ask for both. Comparing them properly stops you from over-practising for the wrong number.

What the adverts ask for

  • ECP Data Entry Operator, BS-14. A screening typing speed of at least 40 WPM in English, followed by a typing test with marks for English and Urdu.
  • A 2022 data entry operator advert. A data entry speed of 10,000 key depressions per hour and a minimum typing speed of 35 WPM.

The ECP position is covered in detail in the ECP data entry operator guide.

What 10,000 KDPH means in WPM

One word counts as five keystrokes, so 10,000 key depressions per hour is 10,000 divided by 300, which is about 33 WPM. In the 2022 advert, the 35 WPM figure was therefore the slightly stricter of the two. The conversion is explained with a table in WPM, CPM and KPH explained.

Why a KDPH test can feel harder

  • It may include digits, codes, dates and symbols, which slow most people down.
  • Entry may be spread across fields, so you press Tab or Enter between entries.
  • Accuracy is usually checked closely, because a wrong digit changes a record.

How to prepare

  • Practise plain typing until your net speed is at or above the advert's WPM figure, using a 15-minute routine.
  • Add number practice with numbers and dates passages.
  • If the job uses the numeric keypad, see the 10-key guide and use a full keyboard.
  • Check your accuracy on every run, since many data entry tests have strict limits.

Frequently asked questions

How many WPM is 10,000 KDPH?

About 33 words per minute, because one word counts as five keystrokes and you divide KDPH by 300.

What typing speed does an ECP data entry operator need?

The advert we reviewed asked for a screening speed of at least 40 WPM in English, followed by a typing test with English and Urdu marks.

Why do some adverts ask for both WPM and KDPH?

They may test ordinary typing and data entry separately. Check your advert to see which figure applies to which part.
