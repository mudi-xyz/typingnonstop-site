Typing test pass marks: Pakistan, India, USA and Canada compared

Typing requirements differ widely between jobs and countries. The figures below come from public job adverts and notices. They can change with every advert, so use them as a guide and confirm with the official notice.

What the table tells you

Most clerical typing requirements sit between 25 and 40 WPM, and many add an accuracy rule. The higher figures tend to belong to data-entry and civil-service typist roles. If you are unsure which level to train for, 40 WPM at 95 percent accuracy covers most of the posts above.

Not listed here

Frequently asked questions

Which country has the highest typing requirement?

Among the examples above, 40 WPM appears in India, the USA, Canada and Pakistan, so the level is similar. The difference is usually in the accuracy rules.

Do these numbers change?

Yes. Each advert can set its own requirement, so always check the notice for your post.

How can I practise for several posts at once?

Train to 40 WPM with high accuracy, then practise the exact format and time limit of your target test.
