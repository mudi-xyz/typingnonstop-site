Questions

Is TypingNonStop free?

Yes. You can practise as a guest without an account. An account lets you keep progress across devices and use the leaderboard and friend races.

Where do the pass marks come from?

From public job adverts and notices, such as SSC, KP and Punjab public-sector adverts, the Election Commission of Pakistan, Delhi High Court, CalHR and Job Bank postings. They change with every advert, so always check the official notice.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, but typing practice is best on a laptop or desktop keyboard.
